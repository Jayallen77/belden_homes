How it works

A clear path from floor plan to move-in.

The old site described a typical process of roughly four months, depending on financing, permits, site work, factory timing, and final completion. Belden should confirm timing for each project.

1

Choose your home

Browse inventory, compare floor plans, and narrow the model based on needs, budget, and site.

2

Confirm site and budget

Review land, financing, driveway, septic, well, electric, foundation, and other setup needs.

3

Finalize and order

Choose options, confirm specs, and place the order. Older site notes referenced 10% down to order; confirm current terms with Belden.

4

Prepare the site

After permit/order steps, site improvements may begin: foundation, driveway, utilities, septic, and more.

5

Delivery and setup

The home is delivered, installed, finished, inspected, and prepared for move-in.

Financing notes

Modular homes are often financed similarly to stick-built homes, including land-home packages or construction-style loans. Manufactured home financing can vary based on whether the home is on owned, rented, or leased land and what foundation is used.

Financing contacts from the legacy site should be verified before production launch.

Bring these details

Land status, rough budget, desired bedroom count, preferred home type, site address, and any existing well/septic/driveway/electric information.
