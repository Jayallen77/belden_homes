Buying process

What to expect.

Each property is different. We’ll work through the home and site details with you before setting a project schedule.

  1. 01

    Choose a home

    Tour models and compare layouts, bedrooms, bathrooms, and available options.

  2. 02

    Review your property & budget

    Bring your site address, land details, and information about existing access, utilities, well, and septic.

  3. 03

    Confirm the details

    Review the home specifications, options, project scope, pricing, and timing with Belden before ordering.

  4. 04

    Prepare the site

    Complete the agreed permits, foundation, access, and utility work so the site is ready for delivery.

  5. 05

    Delivery & setup

    Coordinate delivery, installation, finish work, and required inspections.

Bring to your first visit

  • Your property address
  • Land ownership or purchase details
  • A rough budget
  • Preferred home size and bedroom count
  • Any site plans or utility information

Timing & financing

Timing depends on the home, factory schedule, permits, site conditions, and financing. Confirm your lender’s requirements before committing to a home or site plan.
