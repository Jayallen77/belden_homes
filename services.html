Turnkey Setup

More than a home dealer — Belden helps handle the setup too.

For eligible customers in the service area, Belden can serve as a hands-on local guide through the site work and installation details that come after choosing a home.

Setup work Belden can help coordinate

  • Land preparation
  • Foundation or concrete slab
  • Driveway
  • Well
  • Septic
  • Electrical service
  • Garage, deck, and related improvements
  • Delivery, installation, finish work, and follow-up

The goal

Fewer moving parts for the customer. One experienced local team helping guide the project from order to move-in.

Homes Belden offers

Manufactured / Single Wide

HUD-code homes built on a permanent chassis, typically efficient and budget-conscious.

Double Wide

Manufactured homes built in two sections and joined on-site, usually installed on a slab or foundation.

Modular

Factory-built sections assembled on-site and built to applicable state/local building codes.
