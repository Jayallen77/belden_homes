About Belden Homes

Local, family-run, and helping customers since 1973.

Belden Homes Inc. has operated from Harpursville, New York for more than 50 years, helping customers choose, order, install, finish, and move into manufactured, modular, and double-wide homes.

Built on practical help

Belden’s value is not just selling a home. It is helping buyers understand their options, choose a floor plan, work through financing questions, prepare the land, and coordinate the details needed to get the home set up correctly.

The legacy site emphasizes quality homes, fair pricing, courteous service, clean jobsites, follow-up, and customer satisfaction. This rebuild keeps that message but makes it cleaner and easier to use.

Recognized dealer history

The old site listed Commodore dealer awards from 2004–2008, including Top 5 and Top 10 single-lot dealer recognition. Updated recognition should be confirmed before production launch.

Service area

Belden serves roughly a 50-mile radius around Harpursville, including Broome, Chenango, Delaware, and Otsego Counties in New York. Contact Belden to confirm whether your property is within the current setup area.
