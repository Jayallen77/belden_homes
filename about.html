About Belden Homes

Here in Harpursville since 1973.

Family-owned, with more than 50 years of experience helping local customers choose and set up their homes.

Homes, installation,

Belden Homes was established in 1973 and incorporated in New York State in 1976. Our sales center has been in Harpursville from the beginning.

We work with customers through home selection, ordering, delivery, installation, and finish work. That experience helps us explain what is involved and address the details of each property.

Visit our sales center to meet the team, walk through the homes, and ask questions.
