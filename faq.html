FAQ / Resources

Helpful answers before you visit the lot.

Simple explanations for the questions buyers ask most often.

What is the difference between modular and manufactured?

Modular homes are factory-built in sections and assembled on-site to applicable state/local building codes. Manufactured homes are built to HUD code on a non-removable frame.

Is a double wide a manufactured home?

Yes. A double wide is generally a HUD-code manufactured home built in two sections and joined together on-site.

What is a single wide?

A single wide is a manufactured home built in one section. The legacy site also used “mobile home,” but “manufactured home” is the cleaner modern term.

Can Belden help with site work?

For eligible projects, Belden can help coordinate site preparation such as foundation/slab, driveway, well, septic, electric, delivery, setup, and finish work.

How long does the process take?

The old modular buying page referenced roughly four months total, with factory build time around 4–8 weeks and final completion depending on the home style and site. Timing should be confirmed for each project.

Are homes open for viewing?

The legacy site said houses were open for viewing during daylight hours, but current viewing rules should be confirmed with Belden. Best move: call ahead.

Do prices and specs change?

Yes. Features, colors, specifications, materials, pricing, and availability can change. Confirm current details directly with Belden Homes.
